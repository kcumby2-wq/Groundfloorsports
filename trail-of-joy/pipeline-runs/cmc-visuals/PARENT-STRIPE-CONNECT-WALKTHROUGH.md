# Parent-Facing Stripe Connect Walkthrough (Minor Creator Storefronts)
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active reference doc, written for a non-technical parent. Addresses Gap #5 from the Track A hypothetical run: "Stripe Connect setup for minor creators isn't documented as a walkthrough."

**Who this is for:** A parent/guardian setting up the payout account for their child's SubjectSkillz storefront (cmc-visuals-style). Written in plain language — assume no prior Stripe experience.

---

## The big picture, before any steps

Your child's storefront takes payments from families who book photo/video coverage. That money needs somewhere to go. Because your child is a minor, the payout account is set up **in your name, as the responsible adult** — your child is named in the business description as the creator, but you control the actual financial account. This is standard for any minor-run small business and isn't unique to Stripe.

**What you'll need before starting:**
- Your government ID (for identity verification — standard for any business payment account, not specific to this program)
- Your bank account info (where the money actually lands)
- Your Social Security Number or EIN if you have a business entity already (most parents will use their SSN since this is typically a sole proprietorship-style setup)
- About 15-20 minutes, uninterrupted

## Step-by-step

**Step 1 — You'll receive an invite link** from Subject Medias to set up your child's storefront's payout account. Click it.

**Step 2 — Stripe will ask for your business information.** Enter:
- Business name: typically your child's storefront name (e.g., "CMC Visuals")
- Business type: Individual/Sole Proprietorship (this is the standard choice for this kind of setup — you are not creating a formal corporation)
- Your name as the account holder (not your child's name — you are the legal account holder)

**Step 3 — Identity verification.** Stripe will ask you to verify your identity — typically a photo of your government ID and sometimes a selfie. This is Stripe's standard process for any new payment account, designed to prevent fraud; it's not something Subject Medias added.

**Step 4 — Bank account connection.** Enter the bank account where payouts should land. This is usually your own personal or family bank account, since you're the legal holder of this payout account.

**Step 5 — Tax information.** Since you're the account holder, your SSN (or EIN if you have one) goes here, not your child's. Money flowing through this account is treated as your business's income for tax purposes — **talk to a tax professional about how this should be reported,** especially regarding any portion that functions as compensation to your child for their work (this can have its own tax treatment — a real accountant should advise on the specifics for your situation).

**Step 6 — Review and submit.** Stripe will show you a summary. Double check the business name, your name, and the bank account before submitting.

**Step 7 — Verification period.** Stripe typically reviews new accounts within 1-2 business days. You'll get an email when it's approved.

**Step 8 — Once approved, the storefront is connected.** Money from bookings flows into this account automatically; payouts to your bank happen on Stripe's standard schedule (usually a few days after each transaction, though this can vary).

## Common questions

**"Why isn't this in my child's name?"** Because a minor generally can't be the legal account holder for a financial account like this. You're the responsible adult; your child is the creator and the storefront is branded around them, but the account itself is yours.

**"What if I want to give my child direct access to some of the money?"** That's between you and your child — many families set up a custodial account, an allowance system, or a savings arrangement for the portion that's "theirs." Subject Medias doesn't dictate how you handle that; the 80/20 split (or whatever your specific agreement is) just determines what comes to your account vs. stays with the platform.

**"What if I make a mistake during setup?"** Stripe lets you update most information after the fact through your account dashboard. If something seems wrong or you're not sure, reach out to Subject Medias before submitting — it's easier to get it right the first time than to fix it after money has started flowing.

**"Is this safe?"** Stripe is a major, well-established payment processor used by a large share of online businesses. The identity verification step exists to protect against fraud, not because anything about this specific program is unusual.
