# Parent/Guardian Media & Consent Agreement
**For:** Creator-Owned Coverage Businesses (CMC Visuals and future SubjectSkillz creators)
**Status:** TEMPLATE — to be reviewed with Carson's parent/guardian and signed before stage 6 (Content), stage 7 (Automation), or stage 9 (Portal go-live) proceeds.

---

## Why this document exists

Carson is shooting and selling photo/video coverage of athletes at team events, tournaments, and games. The athlete whose family books and pays for coverage has already agreed to be filmed and have their image sold — that's the transaction. But events involve other people's kids too: teammates in the background, opposing players, siblings, other families at the same field. Those families haven't separately agreed to anything. This document is how Trail of Joy and Carson's parent/guardian agree, in writing, on how that gets handled before any of it becomes a content or automation system that runs without a person checking it.

This is between **Trail of Joy** and **Carson's parent/guardian**. It is not a contract with the other families at events — it's the internal rule Carson follows so that those families' rights are respected.

---

## Section 1 — Who this covers

- **Carson McCauley**, the creator, a minor. Carson's own appearance, name, and likeness in CMC Visuals' marketing and business materials are covered by the standard creator agreement (the parent/guardian-controlled Stripe Connect account already reflects parental control of payouts).
- **Buying families** — the athlete and family who book and pay for coverage. Their consent to be filmed and to have that footage delivered and used for delivery purposes is captured at checkout (existing consent checkbox).
- **Third-party minors** — anyone else visible in event footage who is *not* the paying athlete: teammates, opposing players, siblings, bystanders. **This is the gap this document closes.**

## Section 2 — The rule for third-party minors

1. **Default to private delivery.** Footage that includes recognizable third-party minors is delivered privately to the buying family (download link, private gallery) and is **not** posted publicly (Instagram, TikTok, the CMC Visuals website, portfolio) unless Section 3 is satisfied.
2. **Public posting requires one of:**
   - The shot is a wide/action shot where no other individual child is a clear, identifiable subject (e.g., a stadium-wide action shot, not a posed group photo of another team), or
   - Event/team organizers have a blanket media-release policy covering all participants (common at some tournaments) — Carson or the parent confirms this with the event organizer in advance and keeps a note of which events have it, or
   - The specific third-party family has separately said yes when asked.
3. **When in doubt, don't post it publicly.** Default to the buying family's private delivery.
4. **Team/group photos** for a paying team (the $399 Team Elite package) are different — the team that booked coverage has agreed as a group, so that's covered by the buying family's consent, same as Section 2 wouldn't apply within that team's own purchased content.

## Section 3 — What "confirming with the event/organizer" actually looks like

This doesn't need to be formal every time. In practice:
- Before shooting a new event, Carson or a parent has a 2-minute conversation with the coach/organizer: "Is it okay if I post highlight content from today publicly, or should I keep it to the families who book individual coverage?"
- That answer gets noted (a text, an email, a line in the event record) so there's a record of what was agreed for that event.
- If no one asks, the safe default in Section 2.1 applies: private delivery only.

## Section 4 — What Trail of Joy will and won't automate

- The **checkout consent checkbox** (buying family agrees to be filmed, footage delivered, and used for their own order) stays as-is — that's automated and already live.
- **Public posting of any footage is not automated.** No automation system will auto-post event content to CMC Visuals' public channels. A human (Carson or his parent) reviews and decides what goes public, applying Section 2 each time.
- The content system (stage 6) can produce templates and captions, but the decision of *which specific photos/clips* go public is never delegated to an automated trigger.

## Section 5 — Parent/Guardian sign-off

By signing below, Carson's parent/guardian confirms:
- They understand the distinction between the buying family's consent (covered at checkout) and third-party minors' privacy (covered by this document).
- They agree to the default-private rule in Section 2, and to the organizer-check process in Section 3.
- They understand no automation will auto-publish event content without a human review step.
- They can update or revoke this agreement at any time by notifying Trail of Joy in writing.
