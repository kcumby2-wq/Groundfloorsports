# Capo — Practice Intake Source Material
**Status:** PRACTICE / FAKE. None of this represents a real client. Constructed deliberately as a realistic-but-messy test case to stress-test the CBI agent's schema before it touches a real client. "Capo" here is a constructed fictional brand — a small 7v7 football training program in the DFW area, run by a former college player turned coach.

This is intentionally NOT a clean input. It's the kind of half-finished, partially-contradictory material a real sales call produces. CBI's job is to handle it as-is.

---

## Source 1 — Discovery call transcript (raw, paraphrased from memory after the call)

> Coach Marcus reached out through a referral from one of our 7v7 events. He runs Capo Athletics, a training program in Plano. Says he's been doing it for about 18 months, mostly 8th-12th grade kids, football positional training — DBs and WRs mainly.
>
> What he said he wants: "I need to look more legit. Right now I'm posting from my phone, the videos look like a dad shot them, and the parents who can actually afford the higher-tier training are going to bigger brands. I don't want to BE a bigger brand, I want to LOOK like one and stay myself."
>
> Talked about a few brands he likes — mentioned Parisi Speed School a couple times, said he watches what The Stable Performance is doing on Instagram. Doesn't want to look like "those Florida 7v7 guys with the gold chains and Lambos." His words.
>
> Asked who appears in the content. He said it's mostly his athletes (HS kids), him on camera sometimes, and the gym/field. Said he's "fine being the face when needed" but his clients are the athletes.
>
> Budget conversation went sideways — he's not sure what this should cost, asked for a recommendation. Pushed it to a follow-up.
>
> Timeline: "Yesterday." Doesn't have a hard deadline but wants to be putting better stuff out within 2-3 weeks.

- No brand guide. No type system. No defined photography style.

## Source 5 — Constraints mentioned

- Hard no on anything that looks "flashy" or "money-flexing"
- Doesn't want anyone in the content who isn't an active client (privacy — said parents would be uncomfortable)
- Some of his athletes are under 18 — most are, actually
- Doesn't want to use the word "elite" anywhere because "everyone in this space overuses it"

## Source 6 — Subject-of-imagery note (the part CBI needs to flag on)

Coach Marcus, an adult, appears occasionally. The vast majority of subjects are his athletes — most of whom are minors (he confirmed: most are HS, some middle school). No model release process currently in place for the athletes.
