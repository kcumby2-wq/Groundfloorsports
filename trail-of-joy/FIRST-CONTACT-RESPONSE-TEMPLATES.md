# First-Contact Response Templates & Response-Time Standards
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active reference doc. Addresses Gap #1 from the Track A hypothetical run and Gap #1 from the Track C hypothetical run — both runs found no documented first-contact response template or response-time standard, for two different entry contexts.

---

## Why two templates, not one

Track A and Track C have different first-contact patterns and reasonably different response-time expectations:

- **Track A** entry is typically a parent DMing or messaging after seeing another creator's storefront (Carson-style) or hearing about SubjectSkillz informally. The tone is warm, personal, a little informal — a parent exploring something for their kid.
- **Track C** entry is typically an adult business owner emailing with a B2B inquiry — considered, often somewhat researched already, expecting a professional response cadence.

Using the same template/timing for both would feel wrong in both directions — too stiff for a Track A parent, too casual for a Track C business inquiry.

## Response-time standards

| Context | Standard | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Track A (parent DM/message) | Within 4 hours during business hours; next business day if received evenings/weekends | Trust starts forming the moment a parent reaches out; a fast, warm reply sets the tone for everything that follows |
| Track C (business inquiry, typically email) | Within 1 business day | Matches normal B2B inquiry expectations; faster is good but not required the way it is for a warm parent lead |

## Track A first-contact response template

> Hi [Parent name],
>
> Thanks so much for reaching out! SubjectSkillz is our creator program for young athletes/content creators — [child's name] would run their own small media business (photos/video coverage, their own storefront), with us handling the structure and support, and you staying fully in the loop on everything, including how payments work.
>
> The short version: [child] owns their work, gets a 80/20 split favoring them on storefront sales, and has real safeguards in place around what we will and won't do with their content — happy to walk through all of that in detail.
>
> Want to grab 30 minutes for a quick discovery call? Here's a link to find a time that works: [scheduling link]
>
> Looking forward to it,
> [Name], Subject Medias / Trail of Joy

**Notes on this template:**
- Keep the 80/20 split mention accurate to whatever the real current terms are — don't quote a number that's since changed.
- "Real safeguards" is intentionally vague here; the discovery call is where Section 2-5 of the consent agreement actually gets explained. Don't try to compress the whole consent agreement into a first DM.
- Adjust child/parent name fields; keep the tone warm, not salesy.

## Track C first-contact response template

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for reaching out about creative services for [business name]. Quick overview: we build AI-assisted creative workflows for businesses like yours — covering brand voice/style setup, copy and caption drafts, visual asset generation, and scheduled distribution — with a human review step built into every stage before anything goes live.
>
> If any of your content features minors (athletes, students, etc.), we do have some specific requirements around parent consent and AI-derivative use that we'll walk through — nothing complicated, just important to get right from the start.
>
> Would a 30-minute call work to go over your specific situation and what a good fit would look like? Here's a scheduling link: [link]
>
> Best,
> [Name], Subject Medias

**Notes on this template:**
- The minor-consent mention is intentionally included even in the first response — it's better for a prospective Track C client to know upfront that this is a real consideration, rather than discovering it later in the process and feeling blindsided.
- Don't quote specific pricing in the first-contact response; that's a discovery-call conversation once scope is understood.

## What happens after the first response

Both templates point to a discovery call. From there:
- Track A proceeds per the SCALE-INTAKE-SOP's intake flow.
- Track C proceeds per the Track C Service Agreement's intake flow, including the release-language conversation if minors are involved.
