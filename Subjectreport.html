The Standard·v1.0 · versioned · citable

A number is worthless without its method.

So we published the method. What is measured, how, by whom, and — the part everyone else omits — what the number does not mean.

Part I — What is measured
The fixed five metrics, defined so two graders reach the same call. Nothing that varies with a grade.
Part II — How
Two-angle per-play grading. Play count on every record. Results shown by opponent, never one blended average.
Part III — By whom
Certified graders. The conflict rule: no one grades an athlete they train or are related to. Reassign — never disclose-and-grade.
Part IV — What the number does not mean
  • —Not a prediction of where he'll be placed.
  • —Not comparable across different play counts.
  • —From a single event, it's a data point — not an evaluation.

Questions we get

Answered honestly

Single graded game vs the Full Evaluation?+
A single-game transcript grades one game and hands you the PDF. The Full Player Evaluation ($999) grades a body of film, produces the composite and trend, audits everything a coach asks for, and comes with a four-week plan and a review call. One is a document; this is a diagnosis.
Does the $999 count toward anything?+
It credits in full toward a Verified Season if you start within 30 days of the review call. After that it doesn't — if it credited forever, the evaluation would just be a sales call with an invoice attached.
What if we just want to do it ourselves after the report?+
Good — that's a real outcome, and about four in ten families take it. The report is yours to keep, with the four-week plan, and we check back at thirty days either way.
Who actually grades the film?+
A certified grader inside the Optimum Grading platform, scoring across the fixed five categories, with every grade linked to the exact clip. On family-facing surfaces the grader is identified by number (e.g. Certified Grader #A-11), not name.
Can the number ever be a "not yet"?+
Yes, and sometimes it should be. Some evaluations end with the honest answer that the film doesn't support the level yet. We'd rather tell you that than sell you around it.
Is this NIL-compliant?+
Yes. Agreements are structured to comply with current (2026) NIL guidance. If you're in a state with restrictions, we walk you through what you can and can't do. Your eligibility is not something we put at risk.
