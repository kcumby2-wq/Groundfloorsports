# Coach Onboarding Script

## Goal
Align coaches on what Subjectreport delivers and how to operationalize athlete pipelines.

## 60-second opener
Coach [Name], this is [Your Name] from Subjectreport. I want to walk through how your roster and event data flow into athlete packages, how statuses are tracked, and what your team needs to do weekly.

## Discovery questions
- Are you onboarding one team or multiple programs?
- Which athletes are immediate priority this month?
- Do you want weekly review calls or self-serve dashboard reporting?

## Workflow summary
1. Intake athletes using CSV Builder or direct athlete entry.
2. Assign package and status in admin.
3. Use payment automation events to move athletes from booked to paid to delivered.
4. Review weekly sales dashboard and clear pipeline blockers.

## Coach value points
- Standardized athlete records for cleaner recruiting workflows.
- Faster status visibility for staff and parents.
- Repeatable onboarding templates so every call is consistent.

## Close
If this sounds right, we will provision your admin workflow today and run your first roster through together.

## Internal checklist after call
- Confirm admin access and role assignments.
- Verify CSV Builder walkthrough is complete.
- Save coach communication cadence in notes.
- Set first weekly KPI review date.
