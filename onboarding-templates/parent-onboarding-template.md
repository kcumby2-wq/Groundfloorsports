# Parent Onboarding Script

## Goal
Help a parent understand the package, timeline, and next action in less than 10 minutes.

## 60-second opener
Hi [Parent Name], this is [Your Name] from Subjectreport. Thanks for taking the call. I want to quickly confirm your athlete goals, show what package fits best, and make sure you know exactly what happens after payment.

## Discovery questions
- What is your athlete's primary goal right now: recruiting visibility, evaluation, or both?
- What timeline are you working against: upcoming camps, season, or coach outreach dates?
- Do you already have game film and verified athlete contact info ready?

## Package positioning
- Player Transcript ($249): best for baseline evaluation and immediate recruiting conversation starter.
- Recruiting Program ($1,500): best for athletes needing structured recruiting support and follow-through.
- Full Athlete Package ($5,000): best for full-service positioning and long-term execution.
- Prospect Membership ($99/mo): ongoing access and updates for active subscribers.

## Objection handling
- Too expensive: Focus on timeline and opportunity cost of delayed recruiting outreach.
- Need to think about it: Offer a specific follow-up date and send one clear summary email.
- Not sure which package: Recommend one based on timeline urgency and current film readiness.

## Close
Based on what you shared, I recommend [Package]. Once payment is complete, we move your athlete to paid status, confirm onboarding, and start delivery.

## Internal checklist after call
- Set athlete status to booked or paid in admin.
- Add notes with goals, timeline, and package recommendation.
- If paid, trigger payment automation event and confirm onboarding checklist items.
- Schedule first deliverable target date.
